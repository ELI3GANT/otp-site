# OTP client acquisition playbook

The goal is specific outreach that earns permission to show a fix. The sequence is:

**specific observation → business impact → simple solution → permission to show the fix**

## Prospect lanes

| Client type | Inspect before outreach | Best OTP offer | Pricing guidance | Proof to send |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Contractors and trades | Mobile load, click-to-call, service area, quote form, proof of completed work | OTP Quick Fix or Website & brand | Quote first; focused fixes can fit the $250–$750 guidance band when the scope is small, then route through The Signal/System | HYH website transformation |
| Barbers and salons | Booking link, hours, service menu, Instagram-to-booking path, mobile readability | OTP Quick Fix or focused production | Quote first; use The Signal for one clear output | Homepage/site-flow proof or HYH |
| Auto businesses | Phone CTA, inventory/service pages, map/local relevance, lead response path | Website & brand or OTP Quick Fix | Quote first; package depth follows the number of pages and workflow pieces | HYH or OTP OS systems proof |
| Restaurants and hospitality | Menu access, reservation path, event promotion, Google profile, mobile speed | Focused production or connected launch | The Signal for one asset; The Engine for a connected launch | Song Wars event campaign |
| Professional services | Trust proof, service clarity, intake form, contact response expectation | Website & brand or consultant audit | Consultant audit stays in FIXLINE; execution routes into general booking | HYH and OTP OS |
| Creators and artists | Release destination, visual identity, listening path, campaign timing | Artist rollout | The Signal for one asset; The Engine for a connected rollout | PROTOCOL |
| Small local companies | Public offer, contact path, proof, mobile layout, source of leads | OTP Quick Fix | Quote first; never promise a fixed price before review | HYH or WeatherOS for product clarity |
| Remote businesses with obvious digital problems | Broken form, unclear offer, weak landing page, disconnected tools | Website & brand or The System | The Engine/System when multiple pieces or operations are involved | OTP OS and WeatherOS |

## Outreach checklist

1. Inspect the public website and one active social profile.
2. Identify one observable problem that affects inquiry, trust, or follow-up.
3. Choose the smallest offer that can solve or clarify it.
4. Write one personalized message.
5. Send it and log the source, observation, offer, and next follow-up in OTP OS.
6. Follow up with a useful detail, not a generic bump.

## Message templates

### Instagram DM

> Hey [name] — I noticed [specific observation] on [site/profile]. That can make it harder for [customers/clients] to [business impact]. I’d fix it with [simple solution]. Want me to send the two-minute version of what I would change?

### Cold email

**Subject:** One quick improvement for [business]

> Hi [name], I was looking at [specific page/path] and noticed [specific observation]. The likely cost is [business impact]. OTP helps small businesses clean up websites, booking paths, and connected digital systems. I can show the smallest useful fix first. Open to seeing it?

### Warm referral

> [Referrer] mentioned you may be working on [project/business]. I noticed [specific observation] and have a simple way to improve it. If useful, I can send a short recommendation and let you decide whether a project makes sense.

### Follow-up #1

> Quick follow-up with one concrete detail: [specific fix]. It would give [business] a clearer path from [traffic/source] to [contact/booking]. Want the short walkthrough?

### Follow-up #2

> Closing the loop here. If [problem] is already handled, no worries. If it is still costing attention or inquiries, I can scope the smallest repair and show you what it would involve.

### Interested lead response

> Great. I’ll keep this focused. I’ll confirm the page or flow, the desired outcome, timing, and any references. Then I’ll recommend the cleanest next step: Quick Fix, The Signal, The Engine, The System, or a FIXLINE review if the need is diagnostic.

### Quote/proposal transition

> Based on what you shared, this is closest to [offer/package]. I’ll turn the confirmed scope into a quote with deliverables, timing, ownership, and the next approval step. No payment is collected until the scope is clear.

## Offer separation

- **OTP Quick Fix / Digital Fix:** low-friction entry for one focused website, mobile, booking/contact-flow, landing-page, or small automation repair. It is quote-first and routes into existing Signal/System delivery when appropriate.
- **The Signal:** focused creative output.
- **The Engine:** connected project build.
- **The System:** full operating layer.
- **FIXLINE:** separate diagnostic review and audit intake only.
