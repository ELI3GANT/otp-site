FROM INQUIRY TO DELIVERY

A useful brief starts with four things.

  1. Your current website, if you have one.
  2. The customer you want to reach and the service you want to sell.
  3. What is broken, unclear, or missing today.
  4. Your budget range and desired timeline.

OTP reviews the request, proposes a scope, and confirms the work with you. Delivery details, revisions, and any payment schedule are agreed for the project.

Before you request a quote

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. Describe the problem and include the current URL. A focused improvement may be enough; the scope depends on the existing platform and what needs changing.

Does a new website guarantee Google rankings?

No. Clear content, crawlable pages, mobile usability, and relevant links create a useful foundation. Search visibility also depends on competition, the value of the content, and time. Rankings and lead volume are not guaranteed.

How much does a website cost?

The quote depends on pages, content, integrations, and the condition of an existing site. Share a budget range so OTP can propose an appropriate scope before you commit.

Can the website connect to a booking workflow?

Booking and intake flows are part of OTP’s website and business-system work. Describe your current tools and what should happen after a customer submits an inquiry.
