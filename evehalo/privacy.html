EVEHALO Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 · OnlyTruePerspective LLC

1. Our Core Principle: Local Sovereignty

OnlyTruePerspective LLC ("we", "us", or "our") built EVEHALO under an uncompromising conviction: your thoughts, schedules, habits, and private reflections belong exclusively to you. EVEHALO operates as a sovereign local system on your Apple hardware. We do not sell data, we do not host advertising, and we do not monitor your personal life.

2. Information Collection and Storage

100% On-Device Persistence: All information created in EVEHALO—including tasks, habits, milestones, routines, spaces, and EVO memory notes—is stored locally in Apple’s SwiftData framework on your device. This data is physically isolated to the application container.

No Telemetry or Third-Party SDKs: EVEHALO does not bundle advertising trackers, behavioral analytics, or third-party tracking scripts.

3. Speech Recognition and Audio Processing

EVO's voice features utilize Apple’s SFSpeechRecognizer and AVSpeechSynthesizer frameworks with requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true.

  • Microphone permissions are requested only when voice interaction is initiated.
  • Audio buffers are transcribed locally by the Apple Neural Engine.
  • Raw audio is never transmitted to, stored on, or analyzed by any remote server.

4. System Permissions and Integrations

All integrations are governed by standard iOS permission dialogues:

  • Apple Calendar: Read-only access to display your events within Horizon. No calendar entries are created or deleted.
  • Apple Reminders: Synchronizes with your Reminders lists to provide seamless capture and search capabilities.
  • Notifications: Local notification scheduling for morning/evening check-ins and focus session completion. Notifications are generated on-device without remote push servers.

5. In-App Purchases and Financial Details

Plus Membership purchases (Monthly, Yearly, or Lifetime) are processed directly by Apple via the App Store and StoreKit. OnlyTruePerspective LLC never receives, stores, or processes your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or payment credentials.
