EVEHALO Support Center

Everything you need to know about setting up, troubleshooting, and mastering your EVEHALO personal operating system.

Where is my personal data stored?

All tasks, routine trackers, goals, spaces, and personal memories are stored physically on your iPhone using Apple’s local SwiftData framework. There is no external cloud database, no user profile tracking, and zero data mining.

How does EVO voice recognition maintain privacy?

EVO utilizes Apple’s native SFSpeechRecognizer with requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true. Your speech audio is converted to text entirely by the Neural Engine inside your iPhone's Apple Silicon processor. No audio bytes or speech recordings are ever uploaded to cloud servers.

How does Apple Reminders and Calendar integration work?

Calendar access is strictly read-only to pull your scheduled events into the Horizon timeline without risking mutations. Apple Reminders integration allows two-way synchronization so you can manage your native Reminders lists within the EVEHALO interface. Permissions are completely optional and can be toggled anytime in iOS Settings.

How do I export or back up my database?

In EVEHALO, navigate to Settings → Data & Sovereignty. Tap Export JSON Backup to generate a complete, unencrypted human-readable file that can be saved to Apple Files, iCloud Drive, or sent to your computer. You can restore your data anytime using the Import tool.

What devices and iOS versions are supported?

EVEHALO requires an iPhone running iOS 26.0 or newer. It is optimized for the latest iPhone 18 Pro family (including 120Hz ProMotion and Dynamic Island Live Activities) and features adaptive regular-width dual-column layouts for expanded devices such as the iPhone Duo.

How does Plus Membership work?

All essential planning, Horizon calendar views, EVO voice assistant, and personal space features are 100% free forever. Plus Membership unlocks eight additional handcrafted cosmic atmospheres (Aurora, Crimson, Serenity, Nebula, Solar Flare, Rosewood, Arctic Glass, and Electric Garden). Available as Monthly ($4.99/mo), Yearly ($39.99/yr), or Lifetime ($79.99 once) via standard Apple In-App Purchase.
