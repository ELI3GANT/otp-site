WeatherOS Privacy Policy

Effective Date & Last Verified: August 20, 2026

⚡ Privacy Fast Facts

WeatherOS is engineered by OnlyTruePerspective LLC with a strict privacy-first architecture. We believe weather data should inform your day without compromising your personal privacy.

🛡️
0 Ads & Tracking

No advertising SDKs, no behavioral tracking, and no third-party marketing pixels.

🚫
0 Data Sales

We never sell, rent, monetize, or broker your location or usage data.

📍
Ephemeral GPS

Location coordinates are used strictly in real-time to fetch local forecasts and are never tied to your identity.

💾
Local-Only Cache

Forecasts are cached strictly on your device for responsiveness; no remote user database exists.

1. Company & Product Ownership

This Privacy Policy governs WeatherOS (the "Application"), an official cross-platform application developed, owned, and published by OnlyTruePerspective LLC ("Company", "we", "us", or "our").

Company: OnlyTruePerspective LLC

Product: WeatherOS

Application ID / Bundle: app.weatheros.app

Ecosystem Domain: https://www.onlytrueperspective.tech

Inquiries & Data Privacy Officer: contact@onlytrueperspective.tech

WeatherOS provides a cinematic atmospheric observatory interface on iOS and Android. No user account, registration, login, or personal profile is required to use the Application.

2. Data Collection & Processing

We process only the minimum technical data necessary to deliver real-time meteorological forecasts and atmospheric visualizations:

A. Location Data

When you grant permission, WeatherOS accesses your device's geographical coordinates (latitude and longitude). This data is processed as follows:

  • Purpose: Used solely to resolve your local weather conditions, radar status, hourly temperature trends, and precipitation metrics.
  • Processing Mode: Location resolution happens directly on-device. The coordinates are transmitted via encrypted HTTPS requests to our meteorological data provider to fetch corresponding weather data.
  • No Tracking & No User Association: Coordinates are not stored on Company servers, are not linked to any personal name or identifier, and are never retained after the forecast query is fulfilled.
  • No Location History: WeatherOS does not build travel profiles, background location breadcrumbs, or historical movement tracking.

B. Network & Meteorological API Requests

To supply live atmospheric conditions, the Application transmits outbound HTTPS requests containing your coordinates to retrieve public meteorological data. These requests do not contain personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, device serial numbers, or advertising identifiers (such as IDFA or GAID).

C. What We Do NOT Collect

  • No Identifiers: We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or social media accounts in the Application.
  • No Financial Data: We do not process payments or collect payment card data in WeatherOS.
  • No Behavioral Profiling: We do not monitor cross-app behavior or build advertising profiles.
  • No Unnecessary Sensor Access: We do not access camera, microphone, contacts, health data, or local photos.

3. Platform Permissions Breakdown

WeatherOS requests only the minimum operating system permissions necessary to fetch weather forecasts. Below is the complete declaration for Google Play and the Apple App Store:

Android OS Permissions (Google Play)

Permission Classification Operational Purpose
android.permission.INTERNET Normal Required to communicate with real-time meteorological servers and download live forecasts.
android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Dangerous (Runtime) Used strictly while the app is active to obtain high-accuracy GPS coordinates for precise neighborhood weather conditions.
android.permission.ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Dangerous (Runtime) Allows the application to approximate city-level or regional forecasts when approximate accuracy is preferred by the user.

iOS Permissions (Apple App Store)

Permission Key Usage Description (Info.plist) Operational Purpose
NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription "WeatherOS uses your location to display precise local atmospheric conditions and forecast lenses." Resolves active GPS coordinates to display current atmospheric metrics while you interact with the app.
NSLocationAlwaysAndWhenInUseUsageDescription "WeatherOS uses your location to display precise local atmospheric conditions and forecast lenses." Ensures seamless forecast recovery and widget/lens consistency when returning to the application.

4. Third-Party Meteorological Services

WeatherOS utilizes Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) as its primary open meteorological data transport.

  • Encrypted Transport: All calls to Open-Meteo are transmitted via standard secure HTTPS (TLS 1.3) protocols.
  • Zero Personal Identification: Queries contain solely mathematical coordinate pairs (latitude and longitude) and weather parameter flags (temperature, precipitation, wind speed, pressure). No personal user data or advertising identifiers are shared.
  • Privacy Standard: Open-Meteo does not require user registration or API keys and does not track individual mobile app users.

5. Data Storage, Caching & Retention

WeatherOS does not maintain a remote user database or backend server storing your personal records.

On-Device Local Storage:

  • Framework: SharedPreferences (Android) and UserDefaults (iOS).
  • Stored Content: Local application configuration (e.g., preferred temperature units in Fahrenheit/Celsius, wind speed units in mph/kmh) and short-lived meteorological forecast snapshots.
  • Purpose: Provides offline resilience, instant first-frame rendering, and reduced network bandwidth.
  • Deletion: All locally cached data is destroyed when you clear app data in system settings or uninstall WeatherOS from your device.

6. No Advertising, No Data Selling, No Profiling

OnlyTruePerspective LLC adheres to a strict anti-commercialization policy regarding user data:

  • No Data Sales: We do not sell, rent, monetize, release, disclose, or trade your location coordinates or usage habits to data brokers, advertising exchanges, or commercial entities under any circumstances.
  • No Targeted Ads: WeatherOS does not include third-party advertising SDKs (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin).
  • No User Profiling: We do not combine location queries with third-party datasets to build behavioral or demographic profiles.

7. Security & Encryption Standards

We enforce robust technical measures to safeguard the integrity of data in transit:

  • Transport Layer Security: All external network communications are enforced using modern TLS encryption (HTTPS). Plaintext HTTP requests are blocked by platform network security configurations.
  • Sandboxed Storage: On-device configuration data is restricted to the Application's private sandboxed directory, preventing unauthorized access by other applications on your device.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Standards)

WeatherOS is a general-audience atmospheric utility application. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction), in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU GDPR, and Apple / Google Play child safety regulations. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we may take appropriate steps.

9. User Rights & Permission Controls

You retain absolute control over your device permissions at all times. You may grant, restrict, or revoke location permissions at any time through your operating system settings:

How to Revoke Location Permissions:

  • Android: Open Settings → Apps → WeatherOS → Permissions → Location, then select "Don't allow" or "Ask every time".
  • iOS: Open Settings → WeatherOS → Location, then select "Never" or "Ask Next Time Or When I Share".

Note: Revoking location permissions will disable automatic local weather detection; you can still inspect predefined observatory locations or showcase environments.
