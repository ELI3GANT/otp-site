Frequently Asked Questions

WeatherOS requests location permissions strictly to query current atmospheric data for your immediate coordinates. We adhere to a 100% Privacy Guarantee:

  • Ephemeral Query: Coordinates are used in-memory solely to fetch local weather models and are never written to external servers or user profile databases.
  • Zero Background Tracking: WeatherOS does not track or log your movement while the app is closed.
  • No Data Brokers or Advertising: We do not embed ad SDKs, analytics tracking beacons, or commercial data selling hooks.

Read our full 10/10 Gold Standard Privacy Policy for comprehensive details.

WeatherOS allows dynamic 1-tap unit conversion:

  • Quick Tap: Tap directly on the primary temperature readout on the main dashboard.
  • Preferences Menu: Open the top-right Settings drawer to switch between Imperial (°F, mph, inHg) and Metric (°C, km/h, hPa).

Your preference is stored locally in device sandbox memory and applies instantly across the 7-day outlook, wind speed telemetry, and barometric pressure gauges.

The 7-Day Thermal Spectrum card displays a visual high/low temperature bar with relative color gradients:

  • Gradient Spectrum: Sub-freezing temperatures shift from deep glacial blue to cyan, while summer highs shift from amber to thermal crimson.
  • Precipitation Indicators: Days with rainfall or snowfall probability show an integrated moisture glyph with percentage probability.
  • Daily Expansion: Tap any day in the outlook to expand hourly intervals, dew point curves, and cloud cover metrics.

When severe atmospheric anomalies are detected by NOAA / National Weather Service feeds, WeatherOS highlights active threats in an integrated priority matrix:

  • Threat Categories: Tornadic rotation, damaging wind gusts, severe hail, flash flooding, and extreme heat/cold.
  • Severity Levels: Categorized dynamically as ADVISORY (Yellow), WATCH (Amber), or WARNING (Crimson).
  • Detailed Briefing: Tap any severe threat banner to view emergency safety recommendations, expiration timestamps, and meteorological storm vectors.

Because your $0.99 transaction is processed directly through Apple or Google, refunds are managed according to platform store guidelines:

  • Apple App Store (iOS): Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, select "Request a refund", and choose WeatherOS.
  • Google Play Store (Android): Open Google Play, tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & order history, locate WeatherOS, and select "Request a refund" (available within 48 hours of purchase).
