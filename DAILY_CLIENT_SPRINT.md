# Daily client sprint

These are activity goals, not guaranteed conversion rates.

## Daily target

**50 qualified prospects → 10 conversations → 3 quotes/proposals → 1+ close**

| Lane | Count | What to look for |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Warm contacts | 10 | Previous clients, collaborators, referrals, people who already know OTP |
| Local businesses | 20 | Rhode Island and Massachusetts businesses with a visible digital problem |
| Social prospects | 10 | Instagram, creator, artist, event, and community accounts with a clear need |
| Remote or niche prospects | 10 | Businesses with obvious website, booking, workflow, or automation friction |

## Lead loop

For every prospect:

1. Inspect the public site and the most relevant social/profile surface.
2. Identify one real problem and the business impact.
3. Choose the smallest appropriate offer.
4. Personalize the message around the observation.
5. Send the message.
6. Log business, contact, website/social, niche, location, observation, offer, channel, date, status, next follow-up, estimated value, source, and notes in OTP OS.
7. Schedule the next follow-up before moving on.

Use these statuses: `NEW`, `RESEARCHED`, `CONTACTED`, `REPLIED`, `QUALIFIED`, `BOOKED`, `PROPOSAL SENT`, `WON`, `LOST`, `FOLLOW-UP`.

## 24-hour client sprint

### Hour 1: select the list

Choose 10 warm contacts, 10 local businesses, and 5 social or remote prospects. Remove anyone without an observable problem or a legitimate contact path.

### Hours 2–3: inspect and personalize

Write one evidence-based observation for each prospect. Pick Quick Fix, Signal, Engine, System, or FIXLINE only when the lane matches the problem.

### Hours 4–5: send

Send the first 25 messages across at least two channels. Use a source label such as `direct-outreach`, `local-outreach`, or `referral` in the booking link.

### Hours 6–8: respond and scope

Answer replies personally, ask only questions that change scope, and move qualified leads to `/bookings` with the most relevant source and service parameters.

### Hours 9–24: follow up and quote

Run follow-up #1 for the prior list, send up to three clear quotes or proposal transitions, and record the next action for every open lead. Do not count a message as a conversation until the prospect replies.

## End-of-day review

- Which observation earned a reply?
- Which offer got a qualified booking?
- Which source produced the strongest lead?
- Which question blocked a quote?
- Which follow-ups are due tomorrow?
