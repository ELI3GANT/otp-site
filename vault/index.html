Architecture & Fluidity

COMING IN 1.1

Engineered for
120 FPS fluidity.

Large uncompressed music archives demand uncompromising UI responsiveness. VAULT 1.1 is architected to eliminate unnecessary view redraws and memory overhead across the board.

  • Clock Isolation: High-frequency playback scrubber updates run on isolated display links, ensuring parent view hierarchies never re-render unnecessarily.
  • Display-Scaled Artwork: Embedded high-resolution cover art is decoded and downsampled directly to display points to minimize memory footprint.
  • Hardware Canvas Scrubber: Waveforms render through optimized Canvas routines rather than heavy view stacks.
  • Lifecycle-Aware Loops: Visual processing pauses when VAULT is inactive or backgrounded, conserving battery and thermal headroom.

Local-first privacy

Your catalog stays yours.

VAULT 1.0 does not require a VAULT account or an OnlyTruePerspective backend for its core music workflow. Imported music is stored locally in the app sandbox, works offline, and is not automatically uploaded to OnlyTruePerspective servers.

VAULT includes no OTP analytics collection, advertising, or tracking. Read the VAULT Privacy Policy ↗

Backup and migration

Take your archive with you.

Export and restore portable .vaultbackup archives when you choose. The backup workflow validates file integrity with SHA-256 integrity verification before restoration.

Your chosen export or share destination is handled by the system service or app you select.
