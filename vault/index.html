Local-first privacy

Your catalog stays yours.

VAULT 1.0 does not require a VAULT account or an OnlyTruePerspective backend for its core music workflow. Imported music is stored locally in the app sandbox, works offline, and is not automatically uploaded to OnlyTruePerspective servers.

VAULT includes no OTP analytics collection, advertising, or tracking. Read the VAULT Privacy Policy ↗

Backup and migration

Take your archive with you.

Export and restore portable .vaultbackup files when you choose. The shipping backup workflow validates file integrity with SHA-256 checks before restoration.

Your chosen export or share destination is handled by the system service or app you select.
