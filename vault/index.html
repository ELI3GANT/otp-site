Local-first privacy

Your catalog stays yours.

VAULT 1.0 does not require a VAULT account or an OnlyTruePerspective backend for its core music workflow. Imported music is stored locally in the app sandbox, works offline, and is not automatically uploaded to OnlyTruePerspective servers.

No mandatory account. No ads. No OTP analytics. No automatic music uploads. Core workflows work offline.

Export and restore portable .vaultbackup archives when you choose. The backup workflow validates file integrity with SHA-256 integrity verification before restoration.
