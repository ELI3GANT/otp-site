VAULT Support

A little help keeping your music close.

Import your music

Use VAULT’s import controls to choose audio files from Files or a sharing destination. Keep an original copy of your recordings somewhere safe.

Keep versions together

Open a song to manage its versions and related notes. If a file will not play, confirm that the original audio file plays in another app and try importing it again.

Back up before making changes

Export a .vaultbackup from VAULT and save it outside the app. Keep a second copy of your source recordings. Removing VAULT can remove its local catalog; OnlyTruePerspective cannot recover a catalog from a server.

Get in touch

Email contact@onlytrueperspective.tech with your VAULT version, iPhone model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue. Please do not send private music, device backups, or passwords.

Privacy

Read the VAULT privacy policy for information about local storage, exports, permissions, and Apple services.
