01 / What we look at

The view from
the outside.

Website

Can people find their next step?

Your message, page structure, mobile experience, and the path from interest to inquiry.

Social presence

Does the story hold together?

Public profiles, content, and links: how clearly they represent your work and connect to your offer.

Public listings

Is the essential information clear?

The business details and destinations a potential customer encounters while looking you up.

02 / What you take away

Perspective.
Then priorities.

  1. 01

    Observations

    Clear notes on the public-facing experience, tied to the pages and profiles reviewed.

  2. 02

    Priorities

    Recommended improvements in a practical order, with context for why each matters.

  3. 03

    Next steps

    A direction you can act on. Any design, development, or implementation is scoped separately.

03 / How it starts

Your context
comes first.

  1. Send your public links.

    Use FIXLINE to share your website, social profiles, or listings, along with what you want to improve.

  2. Confirm the review.

    OTP reviews your request and confirms scope, pricing, and timing before work begins.

  3. Decide what happens next.

    Use the findings to choose your next move. Further work requires a separate agreement.

04 / A clear scope

Public links.
Human judgment.

No account access needed.

Send only publicly accessible links. Please don’t include passwords or private client information. This review covers the visible experience; private analytics, internal systems, and implementation sit outside that scope.
