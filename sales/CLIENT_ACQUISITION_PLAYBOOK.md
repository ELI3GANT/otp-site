# OTP Client Acquisition Playbook

## Ideal first clients

Independent local businesses in Rhode Island and nearby Massachusetts with an active offer and a website or social presence that makes it hard to understand, trust, book, or contact them. Good early fits include service businesses, studios, restaurants, independent retail, personal brands, and creators with a concrete launch or inquiry problem.

Prioritize owners who can approve the work, have a current business goal, can share their existing links, and are open to a focused first scope. Avoid treating a weak website alone as proof of budget or buying intent.

## First offer: The Signal

Focused website design or improvement from $500, scoped to the problem. Typical starting points are one landing or service page, a mobile repair, clearer offer presentation, or a cleaner contact/booking path. Additional pages, original copy or media production, custom integrations, and larger rebuilds are separately scoped. Do not promise rankings, lead volume, or a fixed turnaround before review.

## Qualification

- What does the business sell, and who is the customer?
- What is the customer unable to understand or do today?
- What current website, booking tool, or social presence is involved?
- Who approves the work and provides access/content?
- Is the goal a focused fix, a new page, or a broader system?
- Is the starting range appropriate, and what timing matters?

## Outreach flow

1. Research the business and confirm it is active, local/relevant, and plausibly a fit.
2. Identify one observable issue from public pages; do not make claims about their performance.
3. Send a short permission-based opener. Offer one useful observation, not a free full redesign.
4. If invited, send a concise five-minute audit with one proposed improvement.
5. Invite a short discovery call or direct project request at onlytrueperspective.tech/bookings.
6. Log contact, source, issue, and follow-up date in `LEAD_TRACKER.csv`.
7. Stop after the planned follow-ups or immediately when asked.

## Follow-up cadence

- Day 0: personalized opener.
- Day 3: one brief follow-up with a concrete observation or ask.
- Day 7: final polite close-the-loop message.
- After any reply: respond to their stated need; do not continue the automated cadence.

## Discovery call outline

1. Ask what prompted the conversation.
2. Let the owner explain the business, customer, and current path to contact/book.
3. Confirm the highest-impact problem and how they will judge a useful result.
4. Review constraints: platform/access, content, timing, approver, and budget.
5. Recommend a bounded scope and explain what is outside it.
6. Agree whether OTP should send a proposal or whether the lead is not a fit.

## Close and handoff

Public booking requests enter OTP's existing `/api/bookings/submit` intake and are handed to the canonical OTP OS booking/lead workflow. Review the lead in OTP OS, verify source and contact consent, qualify, then create the scoped quote/proposal with existing OTP OS document tools. Confirm agreement and invoice/payment steps through the existing protected workflow. Begin production only after scope, agreement, and required payment are confirmed. Do not create duplicate lead records or collect payment on the public request form.
