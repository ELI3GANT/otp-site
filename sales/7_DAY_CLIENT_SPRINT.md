# Seven-Day Client Sprint

Use the activity targets as ceilings, not quotas. Relevance and a useful observation matter more than volume. Do not bulk-message, scrape private contact details, or keep contacting someone who declines.

## Day 1 · Set the lane

- Review OTP availability, starting scope, current examples, and The Signal offer.
- Choose two local industries where a better website or booking path has a clear customer benefit.
- Research 10 businesses manually; confirm they are active and record a public source link and one visible issue.
- Prepare a short screen-record audit outline and test the `/bookings?offer=site-audit` path yourself.
- Send up to 5 highly personalized messages; log each one.

## Day 2 · Build a qualified list

- Research 15–20 businesses and select up to 10 that appear to fit.
- Check mobile pages, primary service clarity, proof, and contact/booking routes.
- Send up to 10 personalized DMs/emails total; do not send the same bulk copy.
- Offer an audit before recording or sharing one.

## Day 3 · Deliver useful audits

- Follow up once with Day 1 contacts where appropriate.
- For people who accepted, record a concise screen-share: first impression, one mobile or trust observation, one contact-path observation, one concrete change.
- Send the audit privately and invite a short conversation.
- Add agreed follow-up dates to the tracker.

## Day 4 · Calls and qualification

- Research up to 10 additional businesses and send only well-matched outreach.
- Make up to 5 respectful calls during appropriate business hours; stop if asked.
- Run discovery calls using the playbook outline.
- Qualify scope, access, approver, timing, and budget before estimating.

## Day 5 · Scope and proposal

- Review interested leads in the OTP OS lead/booking workflow.
- Draft a bounded proposal using existing OTP OS documents: outcome, deliverables, exclusions, schedule, price, revisions, and payment milestones.
- Send proposal only after the prospect agrees that a proposal is the right next step.
- Record the proposal date and next follow-up.

## Day 6 · Follow through

- Follow up on open proposals with one useful question; never manufacture urgency.
- For accepted work, route agreement and invoice through OTP OS's existing protected workflow.
- Verify agreement, deposit/payment requirements, assets, and access before scheduling production.
- For no-fit leads, close respectfully and record the reason without unnecessary personal data.

## Day 7 · Review and reset

- Send the final planned follow-up to Day 0 prospects who have not replied; then stop.
- Review replies, qualified conversations, proposals, and booked work. Separate activity counts from outcomes.
- Note which industries and observations led to genuine conversations.
- Choose next week's focus based on evidence, not vanity activity.

## Daily operating rhythm

1. 30 minutes: research and qualify.
2. 45 minutes: prepare individualized observations or accepted audits.
3. 30 minutes: send a small, relevant batch and update the tracker.
4. 30 minutes: replies, calls, proposals, and follow-ups.

The original 20 DMs, 10 emails, and 5 calls per day are optional maximums only after prospect quality, channel rules, and capacity are confirmed. Do not send 35 unsolicited touches just to hit a target.
