# Outreach Templates

Personalize every message with a real detail. Send only appropriate one-to-one outreach and honor opt-outs.

## Instagram DM

Hi [name] — I came across [business] and liked [specific real detail]. I noticed [specific public website/contact issue] may make it harder for customers to [book/contact/understand the offer]. I have one quick idea for improving it. Want me to send it?

Would a short, no-pressure audit be useful? If a website project is on your list, OTP starts focused work with The Signal, scoped from $500.
